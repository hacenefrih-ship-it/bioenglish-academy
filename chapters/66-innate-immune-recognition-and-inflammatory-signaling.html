Innate Immune Recognition and Inflammatory Signaling

Scientific explanation — English

Innate immunity provides rapid defense by recognizing conserved microbial structures and signals of tissue damage. Pattern-recognition receptors, including Toll-like receptors, detect pathogen-associated or damage-associated molecular patterns. Their activation can induce transcription factors such as NF-κB and promote production of inflammatory mediators. Macrophages and dendritic cells release cytokines that recruit or activate other leukocytes. Interleukin-1 beta (IL-1β), IL-6, and tumor necrosis factor (TNF) have overlapping but distinct roles in inflammation. Chemokines help direct leukocyte migration, while vascular changes facilitate immune-cell entry into tissues. The inflammasome is a multiprotein complex that can activate caspase-1 and promote maturation of IL-1β in appropriate contexts. Inflammation is protective when controlled but can injure tissues if excessive or persistent. Experimental interpretation requires distinguishing messenger RNA expression, circulating protein concentration, and functional biological activity.

Explication en français

L’immunité innée assure une défense rapide en reconnaissant des structures microbiennes conservées et des signaux de lésion tissulaire. Les récepteurs de reconnaissance de motifs, notamment les Toll-like receptors, détectent des motifs associés aux agents pathogènes ou aux dommages. Leur activation peut stimuler NF-κB et la production de médiateurs inflammatoires. Les macrophages et les cellules dendritiques sécrètent des cytokines qui recrutent ou activent d’autres leucocytes. L’IL-1β, l’IL-6 et le TNF jouent des rôles partiellement communs mais distincts. Les chimiokines guident la migration des leucocytes et les modifications vasculaires facilitent leur entrée dans les tissus. L’inflammasome peut activer la caspase-1 et favoriser la maturation de l’IL-1β selon le contexte. L’inflammation protège lorsqu’elle est contrôlée, mais peut léser les tissus si elle persiste. Il faut distinguer expression de l’ARNm, concentration protéique et activité biologique.

Self-assessment questions

  1. What do pattern-recognition receptors detect?

    Only antibodies / Microbial or damage-associated patterns / Only glucose

    Show answer and explanation

    Microbial or damage-associated patterns — Ils reconnaissent des motifs associés aux microbes ou aux lésions.

  2. Which transcription factor commonly participates in inflammatory signaling?

    NF-κB / Hemoglobin / Collagen

    Show answer and explanation

    NF-κB — NF-κB régule l’expression de nombreux gènes inflammatoires.

  3. What is a major function of chemokines?

    Guide cell migration / Replicate DNA / Digest lipids

    Show answer and explanation

    Guide cell migration — Les chimiokines participent au recrutement cellulaire.

  4. Which enzyme is commonly activated by canonical inflammasomes?

    Caspase-1 / DNA polymerase / Amylase

    Show answer and explanation

    Caspase-1 — La caspase-1 contribue à la maturation de l’IL-1β.
