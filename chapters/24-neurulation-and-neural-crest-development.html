Neurulation and Neural Crest Development

University-level biology and scientific English lesson, with bilingual explanation and learning activities.

Open the interactive academyAll chapters

Scientific explanation — English

During vertebrate neurulation, the neural plate bends and closes to form the neural tube, the precursor of the central nervous system. The surrounding surface ectoderm closes over the tube. Neural crest cells arise near the neural plate border and migrate to multiple destinations. They contribute to peripheral neurons, Schwann cells, melanocytes, and several craniofacial structures. Proper closure of the neural tube depends on coordinated cell shape changes and tissue interactions. Disruption of closure can cause serious neural tube defects.

Explication en français

Pendant la neurulation des vertébrés, la plaque neurale se replie et se ferme pour former le tube neural, précurseur du système nerveux central. L’ectoderme de surface se referme au-dessus de ce tube. Les cellules de la crête neurale apparaissent près des bords de la plaque et migrent vers de multiples destinations. Elles contribuent aux neurones périphériques, aux cellules de Schwann, aux mélanocytes et à plusieurs structures craniofaciales. La fermeture correcte du tube neural dépend de modifications coordonnées de la forme cellulaire et d’interactions tissulaires. Une fermeture perturbée peut provoquer des anomalies graves.

Learning and communication advice

Self-assessment questions

  1. What does the neural tube primarily give rise to?

    The intestinal lumen / The central nervous system / The liver parenchyma

    Show answer and explanation

    The central nervous system — Le tube neural est à l’origine du système nerveux central.

  2. From which embryonic region do neural crest cells arise?

    From the endodermal gut lumen / From mature erythrocytes / Near the neural plate border

    Show answer and explanation

    Near the neural plate border — Les cellules de la crête neurale naissent aux limites de la plaque neurale.

  3. Which cell type can derive from neural crest cells?

    Schwann cells / Hepatocytes in all cases / Cardiomyocytes exclusively

    Show answer and explanation

    Schwann cells — Les cellules de Schwann sont notamment issues de la crête neurale.

  4. What closes above the developing neural tube?

    The renal capsule / Surface ectoderm / The adult dura mater

    Show answer and explanation

    Surface ectoderm — L’ectoderme de surface se referme au-dessus du tube neural.
